# Torii — the shared metaverse

Torii is a decentralized, open-world metaverse built on open protocols. Identity and
communication run on Nostr; value runs on Bitcoin, Lightning, and Cashu. It is not one
company's platform — it is a network of worlds, each self-hosted and run by its own
operator.

## Worlds and travel

- A "world" is a browser-based 3D space (Three.js + Rapier physics) run by an owner on
  their own infrastructure — their own VPS, their own relay.
- Travel is **open by default**: each world advertises a presence heartbeat on Nostr,
  and players step through a torii gate to hop straight to any online world, carrying
  their identity with them. No central hub, no account to register, no host approval.
- The gateway is a **protocol, not a single implementation** — any Nostr/Bitcoin world
  can speak it, not just Torii.

## Identity

- Your identity is a Nostr keypair (an npub). You own it; no company grants or revokes
  it. It carries your reputation and assets across every world.
- You sign in with a NIP-07 browser extension (the Plebeian Signer). There is no Torii
  login and no account database.

## Value

- Value moves over Bitcoin and Lightning, with Cashu for private bearer e-cash. In
  alpha, money is fake sats.
- Worlds can host auctions, sales, and shops; payments move directly between players
  with no central clearinghouse.
- AI is paid for per request when it is paid for at all — never by a subscription or a
  custodial balance you hand to a platform.

## The two zones of a world

- The **nap-zone** (Non-Aggression Pact) is the safe social and commerce area — no
  combat, no aggression. It is where players meet, trade, and talk, and its edge is a
  curved three-comma (mitsudomoe) shape.
- The **arena** is the competitive space — in Torii Quest it is a real-time physics
  shooter. The two zones sit side by side and never bleed into each other.

## What players can do

- Explore worlds and hop between them through a torii gate.
- Meet other players in nap-zones — multiplayer is live; several players share one
  world in real time.
- Place stickers on surfaces in the nap-zone (a nap-zone-only mechanic).
- Trade through a world's product panel and shops.
- Carry a portable character between worlds.
- Complete creator-scoped objectives ("michi") and earn completion badges.
- (planned) opt in to "live following", so friends can see which world you are in —
  opt-in, revocable, and off by default.
