# Torii — the shared metaverse

Torii is a decentralized, open-world metaverse built on open protocols. Identity and
communication run on Nostr; value runs on Bitcoin, Lightning, and Cashu. It is not
one company's platform — it is a network of worlds, each self-hosted and run by its
own operator.

## Worlds and nodes

- A "world" is a browser-based 3D space (Three.js + Rapier physics) run by an owner
  on their own infrastructure.
- Each world is a "node" in the network. Worlds are not joined by a central hub; they
  connect peer-to-peer.
- The gateway protocol is the wire agreement that lets a player hop from one world
  node to another — spatial travel between owners' worlds without a central platform.

## Identity

- Your identity is a Nostr keypair (an npub). You own it; no company grants or
  revokes it.
- There is no single Torii login or account database. Your npub is who you are across
  every world.

## The nap-zone

- Each world has a safe social and commerce area called the nap-zone, marked by a
  curved three-comma (mitsudomoe) edge. It is the place to meet, trade, and talk
  without combat.

## Value and trade

- Value moves over Bitcoin and Lightning, with Cashu for private, bearer e-cash.
- Worlds can host auctions and sales, letting players trade with each other directly
  — no central clearinghouse.

## What players can do

- Explore worlds and travel between them via the gateway.
- Meet other players in nap-zones, and opt in to "live following" so friends can find
  which world you are in.
- Complete creator-defined objectives ("michi") and earn completion badges.
- Carry a portable character / avatar between worlds.
- Place stickers and decals on surfaces in the nap-zone.
